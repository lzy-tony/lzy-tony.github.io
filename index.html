Biography

I am a Ph.D. student at the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Gao Huang. I received my B.E. degree from the Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University in July, 2024. I am also currently a research intern at Kling Team, Kling AI.

Previously, I was a research intern at Visual Computing Group, Microsoft Research Asia from September, 2023 to May, 2024. I was a research intern at Foundation Model Group, Megvii Research from May, 2023 to August, 2023. I was a research assistant at TSAIL Group from December, 2021 to September, 2022.

Currently, my research interest include topics on deep generative models, unified understanding and generation models, and their applications in Computer Vision and Embodied AI.
